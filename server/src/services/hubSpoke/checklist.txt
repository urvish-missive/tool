Search intent: the page answers the exact question behind the primary keyword in the first screen, and the title promises what the page delivers
Primary keyword in the H1, the first 100 words, at least one H2 and the meta title; secondary keywords used naturally in H2s and body, never stuffed
Experience: at least two first-hand examples, scenarios or lessons from practice, not generic advice
Expertise: every section explains why, not only what; terms are precise and defined on first use
Authoritativeness: every claim that needs proof has a current, authoritative source (last 2 years where possible); nothing is invented
Trust: no unverifiable numbers, no fake quotes or customers; author bio, date and sources are provided for
Depth: each H2 has enough substance to stand alone, with the how and a concrete example
Originality: the page says something competitors on page one do not (an angle, a framework, a step, data)
Structure: H1 once, logical H2/H3 hierarchy that mirrors the outline, scannable paragraphs and lists, no labelled lead-ins ("How it works:", "The benefit:"), a short summary box for long pages
Visuals: at least one image, one infographic or table and one video embed are placed where they add meaning, each with a described purpose and alt text
Internal links: links to the pillar and the sibling pages with descriptive anchor text, placed in context
External links: links to the sources cited, placed where the claim appears
Readability: plain language, short sentences, active voice, no filler phrases, reads well on mobile
Conversion: one clear call to action in context, matching the funnel stage, no hard sell
On-page SEO: meta title under 60 characters, meta description under 155 characters, descriptive URL slug suggested, FAQ answers short enough for snippets
Freshness: dated claims flagged for review and a suggested review date
