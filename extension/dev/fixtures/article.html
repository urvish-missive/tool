How to Build a Content Strategy That Actually Ranks

In today's fast-paced digital landscape, it's no secret that content is king. Let's dive in and unlock the secrets of a strategy that truly moves the needle — because a great strategy is a game-changer.

Last quarter we audited 40 B2B blogs. The ones that grew organic traffic all did one thing: they wrote for a single, specific reader and answered the question that reader typed into Google.

Start with search intent

Here's the thing: every keyword hides a job the searcher wants done. Before you write a word, look at the top results and ask what format and depth they reward.

  • Informational queries want explanations and examples.
  • Commercial queries want comparisons and proof.
  • Transactional queries want a clear next step.

A quick checklist

Map each article to one intent, one audience and one primary keyword. Then read our keyword research guide or click here.

Show first-hand experience

Readers and search engines both reward lived experience. Share the numbers from your own campaigns, the mistakes you made and what you would do differently next time.

The best content explains why something works, not just what to do.
